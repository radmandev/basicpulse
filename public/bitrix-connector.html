BasicPulse WhatsApp

Route incoming WhatsApp messages into a Bitrix24 Open Line, and reply directly from Bitrix24 Chat.

Checking status…
Find it in Bitrix24 → Contact Center → Open Lines → edit a line → the ID is in the URL (e.g. /openlines/config/edit/1/).
How it works
After activation, every incoming WhatsApp message is pushed into the selected Open Line. Agents can reply directly from Bitrix24 Chat and their replies are delivered back to the contact over WhatsApp via SendPulse.
